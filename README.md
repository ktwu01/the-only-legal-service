# The Only Legal Service: A Cross-Disciplinary Inquiry

## The Thesis

> 养猫是世界上绝大多数男性一生中唯一能够合法购买的性服务
>
> *"Keeping a cat is the only 'sexual service' that the vast majority of men in the world can legally purchase in their lifetime."*

## Abstract

This working paper subjects a single sentence to the full apparatus of modern scholarship and reports, with regret and some alarm, that the apparatus survives the encounter less well than the sentence does. Nine disciplines define their terms, weigh the evidence, and render verdicts. Their findings converge on one structure: the empirical premises of the thesis (that the purchase of sexual services is criminalized for roughly nine in ten of the world's approximately 4.1 billion men; that female feline cohabitation is available legally in nearly every jurisdiction on earth for a one-time fee in the range of $0 to $200 plus recurring maintenance; that the companion thereby acquired co-sleeps, solicits touch, and demands affection) are confirmed by every discipline that checked them. The connecting premise, that what is thereby purchased is a *性服务*, is confirmed by none of them under its literal definition, and by all of them under a redefinition built on the English polysemy of *pussy* (a cat, attested since the 16th century; a slang term of the disputed category, attested later). The committee therefore classifies the thesis as empirically sound and semantically load-bearing on a pun, which in this literature is the highest available classification for a joke. The synthesis (Paper 00) records a unanimous result, nine verdicts of *holds with redefinition* out of nine, and notes that the redefinitions, once collected, all point in a single consistent direction that none of the disciplines was willing to state in its own paper.

## Disclaimer

This repository is **satire and humor research**. It is a joke examined with straight-faced scholarly method, which is the entire point of the exercise. Figures are order-of-magnitude estimates drawn from publicly known statistics and are deliberately hedged; nothing here is legal, medical, or financial advice, and no finding in these papers should be cited by anyone, for any purpose, ever. The cat, for her part, was not consulted and would not have cared.
